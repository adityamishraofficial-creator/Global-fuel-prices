Project overview

Understanding the changing cost of fuel

Fuel prices affect household budgets, transport costs, business operations, and national economic activity. Comparing prices across countries and over time helps reveal how market conditions and public policy may contribute to differences.

📈

Price trends over time

Explore how fuel prices change during the 2020–2026 period and identify periods of increase, decrease, or relative stability.

🌍

Country comparison

Compare country-level values to see how the cost of fuel differs across markets and why a single global average can hide local variation.

🧾

Taxation perspective

Consider taxation as one part of the final price, alongside international energy prices, currency movements, and domestic market factors.

Methodology & interpretation

How to read the analysis responsibly

A clear comparison depends on consistent units, time periods, and definitions. Keep the following checks in mind when interpreting the visualizations.

Check the measure

Read each chart title, axis, legend, and tooltip. Confirm the fuel type, currency, unit, and whether a value represents price, tax, or a percentage.

Compare like with like

Check that countries and dates are comparable. Missing values, different tax structures, and changes in reporting can affect interpretation.

Separate pattern from cause

A chart can show that two measures move together, but it does not alone prove that taxation caused a price change. Use contextual evidence for causal claims.

Frequently asked questions

Quick guide

What is the objective of this project?

To explore global fuel-price and taxation trends from 2020 to 2026 through interactive Tableau views and country comparisons.

Why can fuel prices differ between countries?

Differences may reflect taxes, subsidies, exchange rates, transport and distribution costs, local policy, and international supply conditions.

Why is the Tableau view taking time to load?

The visualization is hosted by Tableau Public and needs an internet connection. Refresh the page or open the direct Tableau link if it remains blank. If the direct link also fails, the issue may be with the published view rather than this webpage.

Can I interact with the charts?

Yes, when supported by the published view. Try selecting marks, hovering for tooltips, or using available filters and story navigation.

Does the project prove that taxes cause higher fuel prices?

No. The visualizations can support comparisons and identify patterns, but causal conclusions require additional evidence and context.
